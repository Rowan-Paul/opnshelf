## Keep your Integrations in sync

Trakt, Popfeed and Bingers Sync handle service interruptions more reliably, pausing and retrying instead of sending affected items to Needs attention. Popfeed Sync also protects changes made while a sync is running and stops safely when a record cannot be read.

Saving sync settings keeps eligible Watches in scope. Connecting Popfeed waits when another sync is busy, and sync warning Notifications now include Bingers.

## Clearer feedback when tracking

If an Episode cannot be removed, Opnshelf reports the failure instead of showing it as successfully removed.

## Easier account access on Mobile

The Handle field stays above the keyboard during sign-in. Sign out is now directly accessible from Settings.

**Availability:** v1.7.28.
