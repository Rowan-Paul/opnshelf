## Show what you are watching now

Choose **Start watching** on a movie or episode to check in. Your check-in appears on your profile and in your followers’ Activity Feed, following your Shelf visibility.

When the runtime is over, Opnshelf logs the Watch for you, dated at the moment you started. Choose **I’m done** to log it sooner, or cancel to leave nothing behind. You can have one check-in at a time.

## Find things in your Library

Search your Library by title, Box Set or episode name, and sort it by date added, title or release year. Search and sort combine with the Format filter.

## Notes on List items

Add, edit or remove a note on any item in your Lists. Notes brought over by Trakt Sync are now visible too. The editor tells you who can read a note, based on the List’s visibility.

## Move through a movie collection

A movie that belongs to a collection shows the other movies in release order, with how many you have watched. Season and episode pages have compact previous and next links at the top.

## Everyday improvements

Up Next cards fit wider phone screens, the active tab on a Mobile profile matches its label, and genre links on detail pages are tidier.

**Availability:** Available on Web. On iOS and Android, these improvements require version 1.7.30, which is coming to the App Store and Google Play once review completes.
