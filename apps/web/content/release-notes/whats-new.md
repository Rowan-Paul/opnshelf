## A place for the latest improvements

Open **What’s new** to browse Opnshelf’s Release Notes. Each entry explains what changed and where it is available.

Find it in your account menu on Web or in Settings on Mobile. A small dot lets you know when there is something new to read. Opening the history clears the dot across your signed-in devices.

You can also share a link to an entry — no account is needed to read it.

**Availability:** Available on Web. On iOS and Android, the Release Notes screen requires a compatible app with the production over-the-air update installed. Let the update download, then close and reopen the app to load it.
