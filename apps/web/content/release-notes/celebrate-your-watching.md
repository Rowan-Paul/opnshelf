## See your recent progress

Home and Profiles now show this month’s Watches and your current Watch Streak. Movie and episode Watches count, including rewatches.

## Catch up with friends more easily

Episode Watches in the Activity Feed are grouped by person, Show, and day. Expand a group to see the individual episodes, with compact summaries on Home.

## Celebrate finishing a Season or Show

A burst of confetti marks a newly completed Season or Show. Turn it off in Settings on each device if you prefer; reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Small fixes for everyday tracking

Season posters now prefer English or language-neutral artwork, falling back to the Show poster when needed. On Mobile, Show Shelf actions are available again from People pages.

**Availability:** Web receives these changes with v1.7.10. On iOS and Android, client changes require the production over-the-air update for app version 1.7.2. Let the update download, then close and reopen the app to load it. Season poster improvements come from the shared catalogue on both clients.
