## Change sync settings without starting over

When you save Popfeed or Trakt Sync settings while a sync is running, Opnshelf now waits and retries automatically. Your confirmed settings stay visible while it waits. If the sync stays busy, you can retry the save.

## A clearer unread indicator on Web

The dot for unread Release Notes stays in the right place beside your account or menu button. Tablet layouts show one indicator, and the account menu closes when resizing hides its button.

**Availability:** Available on Web with this release. On iOS and Android, the sync settings improvement requires version 1.7.14 with the production over-the-air update installed. Let the update download, then close and reopen the Mobile App to load it. The unread indicator changes apply to Web.
