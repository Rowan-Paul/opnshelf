Opnshelf 1.6.5 brings Privacy Alpha, Trakt Sync, and improvements to everyday tracking.

## Choose what you share

Privacy Alpha lets you make your Shelf, Library, Notes, and individual Lists private. Manage visibility in Settings, with progress shown while existing records move.

## Keep Trakt in sync

Connect Trakt to sync Watches, Ratings, Lists, watchlist, Favorites, Notes, Library, and Reviews. Choose what transfers and in which direction, follow catch-up progress, and resolve items that need attention.

## Discover and track more easily

Find Featured Content in Discover, change the date of an existing Watch, and see saved Ratings on media detail pages. This release also improves onboarding, navigation, and the release calendar.

## Keep up with What’s new

Find future release notes in your account menu on Web or in Settings on Mobile.

**Availability:** Available on Web. On iOS and Android, these changes require the latest over-the-air update for app version 1.6.4. Let the update download, then close and reopen the app to load it.
