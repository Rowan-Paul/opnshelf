## Bring your history with you

You can now bring your Popfeed Watches, Ratings, Reviews, Lists and watchlist into Opnshelf. Find **Popfeed Sync** in **Integrations** in Settings, or connect it during Onboarding to start with your existing history.

Choose what to bring over and keep it up to date with ongoing sync. You can also send supported records from Opnshelf to Popfeed, or sync in both directions.

Choose what to sync and review conflicts or items that cannot transfer in **Needs attention**. Popfeed records are public, and sending records to Popfeed requires your permission. Some details, including rewatches and episode watch dates, cannot be represented in Popfeed.

## Return to your account more easily

The sign-in screen offers recently used accounts. Permission requests explain what you are granting before taking you to your account’s server. Notification emails also include unsubscribe links that work without signing in.

## Everyday improvements

On Mobile, detail actions fit into one row, with extra options in a **More** sheet. Sheets and dialogs dim the screen behind them. Across clients, people without a display name are shown by their handle, and Up Next orders tied episodes more consistently.

**Availability:** Available on Web. On iOS and Android, these improvements require version 1.7.14, available in the App Store and Google Play. The Android store build also includes a certificate trust security update.
