## Less scrolling on Web and Mobile

Home, Discover and detail pages use space more efficiently. Upcoming is easier to reach on smaller screens, and Your Shelf is a swipeable row on the Web App on phones.

## Keep editing Lists during Trakt Sync

You can edit Lists while Trakt Sync reads your account. Movie release dates and dates on People pages now display the same calendar day across timezones.

**Availability** — Web and the in-app update for iOS and Android.
