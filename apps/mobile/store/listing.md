metadata is also encoded in `store.config.json` for `eas metadata:push`; Google
Play has no EAS equivalent yet, so paste the relevant fields into the Play
Console by hand.

## Full description
Track the movies and TV shows you watch, find your next episode, and keep your Watchlist close.

- Log Watches, choose the date, and save ratings, Notes and Reviews.
- Follow your shows with Up Next and a release calendar.
- Build Lists and keep track of your Library.
- Use Pick for me to find a movie or episodes that fit the time you have.
- Discover featured collections, browse by genre, and see what friends are watching.
- Find Where to Watch on your streaming services.
- Import your Trakt history or connect ongoing Trakt Sync for Watches, ratings, Lists and more.
- Revisit your watching with weekly, monthly and yearly Watch Recaps.

Choose what you share. Privacy Alpha offers visibility controls for your Shelf, Library, Notes and Lists.

Opnshelf is built on the AT Protocol, an open network that lets you keep your identity and media history beyond a single app.
